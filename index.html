03Beginner path: your first hours

A simple order to follow so you don’t lose cash, get stuck or waste time. Everything here comes from the tips and sources listed on this page. Minor spoilers for the opening missions.

  1. Before midnight

    Get ready

    Free up to 212 GB, finish the pre-load, install the day-one patch and (on PS5) the required system update. Check your unlock time in the FAQ below.

  2. Hour 1

    Learn the basics

    Jason’s starting area is the Leonida Keys. Learn Focus mode (click both sticks), check your Health, Stamina and Focus, and eat, sleep and shower to keep them up. Stash cash under the floorboards in Jason’s bedroom until you have a bank account.

  3. Hour 1–2

    Open your bank

    Do the early missions ‘Buck Me’ and ‘Deposit Cash Into BuckMe’: deposit cash at an ATM so a death or arrest doesn’t cost you what you carry.

  4. Early hours

    Earn your first money

    Try low-security shop robberies solo, check payout and security on the map, look for back doors and use a silencer. Sell jewelry, narcotics and marked bills to fences instead of hoarding loot.

  5. Early hours

    Handle the heat

    Police need to identify you. Change outfit, drop visible weapons, switch vehicle, destroy CCTV or split from your partner to lose them.

  6. Chapter 3 and on

    Level up

    Banks wait until Chapter 3 (you need a dye pack jammer). Save per chapter: each one moves you to a new safehouse with no way back without a hard save.

07Tips for day one

  1. Clear space and update first

    Up to 212 GB. On PS5, Sony issued a system software update that is required before playing; install it before midnight on Nov 19 (PlayStation Support via Kotaku). Pre-loaded copies stay locked until midnight local time; a console trick like a New Zealand account is not guaranteed to work (ynet).

  2. Hide cash until you have a bank account

    Before the ‘Buck Me’ mission, stash cash under the loose floorboards in Jason’s bedroom (VGC). Carried cash is lost if you die or are arrested.

  3. Build healthy habits

    Work out, eat, sleep and shower regularly. Focus gains are permanent; health and stamina need upkeep (GameSpot).

  4. Use Focus mode everywhere

    Click both sticks. It highlights enemies (red), interactables (yellow), cash (green), safe-code notes, and tells white (enterable) from red (alarmed) doors.

  5. Plan robberies

    Check the map for payout and security level. Low-security shops are fine solo; medium and high are easier as a pair. Look for back doors, and use silencers.

  6. Skip banks until Chapter 3

    Bank cash carries dye packs; you need a dye pack jammer from a Chapter 3 contact (GameSpot).

  7. Shake the cops by fixing heat indicators

    Swap outfit, drop visible weapons, change vehicle, destroy CCTV, split up with your partner.

  8. Fast travel without a map warp

    RydeMe (about $100 extra to skip), trains and buses, or switch characters.

  9. Save by chapter

    Each chapter moves to a new safehouse and there is no going back without a hard save (GameSpot).

  10. Mind your minimap

    Doors at the back of shops that show on the minimap may be enterable rooms or stairs (VGC).

08Money, Buckme & banking

Buckme is the in-game bank, shown with a purple icon on the HUD; the green wallet icon is cash on hand. It is real in the game, and is a parody of apps like Cash App, Venmo and PayPal (GTA Wiki).

  • Open an account in the early mission ‘Buck Me’; the next mission, ‘Deposit Cash Into BuckMe’, has you deposit cash at an ATM (PowerPyx).
  • You must use an ATM to deposit physical cash; the phone app manages the account but does not bank pocket cash (Madara guide).
  • Cash on you is lost on death or arrest; some checking balance can still be lost on death, but less (PowerPyx).
  • Vendors take pocket cash only up to $999; anything $1,000+ is paid through Buckme. With $0 pocket cash, purchases come straight from Buckme.
  • Family Savings can never be withdrawn but is permanently safe; hitting savings goals unlocks price perks and raises your checking limit.
  • Loot is not money: sell jewelry, narcotics and marked bills to fences (for example Yasmani in Little Cuba pays well for jewelry and marked bills; Tyrone for narcotics). Stolen vehicles also sell, and damage lowers the price.
  • Money-makers: shop and store robberies, armored vans, selling stolen cars, and the opportunities that phone reminders and contacts surface. Some story beats require you to raise a set amount.

Watch out: the @buckme_sol X account is not official

The X account @buckme_sol (bio: “Official GTA 6 bank app”, created Oct 15, 2026, unverified) is an unaffiliated crypto promo, not Rockstar’s. It posts daily about a Solana token and tags streamers. Its own site, buckme.dev, says it is fan-made and not affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two, and its trading game uses fictional coins with simulated prices. Larpcheck scored two of its posts 43/100 and 57/100: the in-game app is real lore, the “official” claim and any tie to a crypto token are not supported by Rockstar or any source we found. GTASpots notes GTA-named tokens are not official. Nothing here is investment advice.

09Vehicles, weapons & collectibles

Vehicles

Car theft is layered: alarms, trackers (standard and advanced that ping police), keyless cars needing a key cloner. Hotwiring works on most others. Register keepers at Pay ’n’ Spray; worse cars cost less.

Stash bags

Two per character; vans and trucks can hold about eight in the trunk, motorcycles none (GameSpot).

Weapons

Robust customization; sights (right-stick click to first-person) and silencers matter most. Your own dropped weapons return to the trunk.

Ultimate Edition

Adds premium vehicles, weapons, apparel and action; exact lists are not itemized on the pages we could read.

Collectibles

A wildlife journal (62 bird species reported) is confirmed. No verified full collectible list yet, so none is given here.

Leaked footage (unconfirmed)

Oct 2026 Cyberleek leaks showed a dirt-bike mud race, tuning and street racing, and a tank in a river. Unconfirmed and from an older build.

12FAQ

When can I play? (unlock times)

Rockstar and PlayStation use a rolling midnight launch: GTA 6 unlocks at 00:00 local time on Thu Nov 19, 2026, region by region. Examples (midnight Nov 19 local = UTC): New Zealand 11:00 UTC on Nov 18 (first), Eastern Europe incl. Israel 00:00 IST = 22:00 UTC on Nov 18, Central Europe 23:00 UTC Nov 18, UK 00:00 UTC Nov 19, US East midnight ET = 05:00 UTC. Other US zones: sources differ, so check your platform’s store listing. Sources: PlayStation Blog, LeonidaLab table.

How big is the download?

Up to 212 GB of free space, per Rockstar (The Verge). Pre-load has been open since Nov 10, and the boxed version contains only a download code, no disc. Day-one patch 01.002.001 is about 602 MB on PS5.

What platforms is it on?

PS5 and PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S. Not PS4. Xbox One can stream via Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1am ET Nov 19 (08:00 Israel) at Series S settings (Xbox Wire).

Is there a PC version?

Not at launch, and no PC release date has been announced. A Q1 2027 PC release is only a rumor (Polygon).

Is there an online mode?

No GTA VI online mode has been announced; GTA 6 launches as a single-player game and existing GTA Online continues. Claims about a 2027 multiplayer launch are expectations, not confirmation.

How much does it cost?

US: Standard $79.99, Ultimate $99.99. Israel list price ₪319, Ultimate upgrade +₪80. Pre-order bonus on PlayStation: Vintage Vice City Pack plus one month of GTA+ (claim by Mar 31, 2027).

What frame rate does it run at?

30 fps target on all consoles. PS5 Pro adds PSSR clarity and ray-traced shadows (Game Informer).

Is the @buckme_sol account official?

No. It is an unaffiliated crypto promo. The Buckme bank app is real in the game; the X account and its Solana token are not from Rockstar. See the Money section.

Will servers crash on launch day?

Rockstar hasn’t issued a statement. The game is single-player, so the main risk is slowdowns on the PlayStation and Xbox stores and logins, per commentary; Rockstar staggered two pre-launch patches to spread load (Polygon).
